02 — HEART

One tool. Every architecture.

HEART — Hybrid-Electric Aircraft Research Tool — is the object-oriented MATLAB design environment I lead. It sizes kerosene, hydrogen fuel-cell and turboelectric regional aircraft on equal footing. Pick an architecture and hover the components.

03 — The sizing loop

Iterate until it converges.

Every discipline feeds the next. The loop closes on take-off mass, while an outer loop stretches the fuselage until the cryogenic LH₂ tank fits — closing the hydrogen geometry–mass coupling. Hover a module.

    04 — Design exploration

    From a thousand designs to one.
